Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making Haveli Restaurant 5 accessible to everyone.

Haveli Restaurant 5 is committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of ability or technology.

Standards

We aim to comply with WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines. Our site includes semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, descriptive alt text on images, sufficient color contrast, and an accessibility menu (look for the person icon on the right side of every page).

Accessibility Features

The accessibility menu lets you adjust text size, enable high contrast mode, reduce motion, and underline links — your preferences persist across pages.

In-Person Accessibility

Our restaurant is wheelchair accessible. Please contact us in advance for any specific accommodations and we will do our best to assist.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on our site or have suggestions, please let us know. We take all feedback seriously and aim to respond promptly.

Updates to This Document

Haveli Restaurant 5 reserves the right to update this document at any time. Material changes will be communicated via the website. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of our services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

Questions or Concerns

If you have any questions about this document or wish to exercise your rights, the fastest way to reach us is via the contact form on this site. We aim to respond within two business days. For in-person inquiries, please refer to the addresses and hours listed on our contact page.

Governing Provisions

This document is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction in which Haveli Restaurant 5 operates. Any disputes will be resolved in the appropriate local courts. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full effect.
